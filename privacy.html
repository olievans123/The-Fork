Privacy & Cookies

Last updated: June 2026

The Fork ("we", "the site") is an independently operated album-review aggregator. This page explains what data is collected when you visit and how it is used.

Data we collect

The Fork does not require an account and does not ask you for personal information. We do not directly store names, email addresses, or payment details. Your filter and search preferences are kept in your browser (local storage and the page URL) and are never sent to us.

Advertising & cookies

This site is supported by advertising served through Google AdSense. To deliver and measure ads, Google and its partners may use cookies and similar technologies, including the DoubleClick cookie, to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites.

  • Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits.
  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You can opt out of personalised advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendor cookies at aboutads.info/choices.

If you are in the EU or UK, an in-product consent message is shown before personalised ads are served, and you can change your choice at any time.

Analytics

We may use privacy-respecting aggregate analytics to understand overall traffic. These do not identify individual visitors.

Third-party links

Reviews and album data link out to third-party sites (such as Pitchfork). Those sites have their own privacy policies, which we do not control.

Your choices

You can clear cookies and local storage at any time through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect ad relevance but will not stop you using the site.
